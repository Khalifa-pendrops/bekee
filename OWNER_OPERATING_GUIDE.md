## 1. How to Access Your Admin on Your Phone

1. Open **Safari** (iPhone) or **Google Chrome** (Android) on your mobile phone.
2. Navigate to your store link and add `/admin` at the end:
   `https://arubekee.com/admin` (or your staging address).
3. Bookmark this page or tap **"Add to Home Screen"** in your phone's browser menu. This creates an app icon on your phone screen for instant 1-tap access!

## 2. How to Add a New Dress, Shoe, or Bag (3 Steps)

Whenever new styles arrive from your Aba cutting and sewing workshop:

1. Tap **"+ Add Product"** at the top right of your screen.
2. **Step 1 (Photos):**
   - Paste or upload lookbook photos of the item.
   - Tap **"Make Cover"** on the photo that should show first on the homepage.
   - Tap *Continue to Step 2*.
3. **Step 2 (Name & Price):**
   - Type the dress or shoe title (e.g., *The Chioma Draped Satin Midi Dress*).
   - Select the Category (*Clothing*, *Shoes*, *Bags*, or *Accessories*).
   - Enter your price in Naira (e.g., `45000`). If you have a genuine sale price, enter it in the Sale Price box; otherwise leave it blank.
   - Tap *Continue to Step 3*.
4. **Step 3 (Sizes & Colours):**
   - Tap to select the colours made (e.g., *Imperial Oxblood*, *Emerald Jewel*).
   - Tap the sizes available (*UK 10, UK 12, UK 14* for dresses, or *EU 38, EU 39, EU 40* for shoes).
   - Enter how many pieces of each size you have in stock in the boxes.
   - Tap **"Publish to Live Store"**.

Your item is immediately visible to shoppers across Nigeria!

## 3. How to Record a Walk-in or WhatsApp Sale (Fast POS)

When a customer walks into your Aba showroom on Ehi Road or chats you on WhatsApp and buys a dress:

1. Tap the gold **"Record Walk-in / WhatsApp"** button (or the bolt icon ⚡ at the bottom of your phone screen).
2. Select whether it's an **Aba Boutique Walk-in** or **WhatsApp Direct Order**.
3. Pick the product and the exact size/colour they bought.
4. Select how they paid: **Cash**, **Store POS Machine**, or **Direct Bank Transfer**.
5. Enter your name (e.g., *Amaka - Manager*) so your inventory log stays clear.
6. Tap **"Confirm Sale & Deduct Stock"**.

> **Why this matters:** This immediately removes the dress from your website inventory so an online customer in Lagos doesn't try to purchase an item you just sold across the counter in Aba!

## 4. How to Pack & Dispatch an Online Order

When an online customer pays via Paystack:

1. Open your admin and tap **"Orders"** at the bottom navigation bar.
2. Orders marked in yellow as **"unfulfilled"** are awaiting packing. Tap **"Manage"** on the order.
3. Review the customer's items, sizes, and landmark:
   - Tap **"Copy Packing Slip"** to share the list with your packaging staff.
   - If you need to clarify their location, tap the green **"Chat on WhatsApp"** button to message them directly.
4. Package the garment into an ARUBEKEE branded box with tissue paper.
5. Take the package to your courier (e.g., **GIG Logistics**, **Peace Mass Waybill**, or an **Aba Dispatch Rider**).
6. In the order screen:
   - Select the courier name.
   - Type the Waybill reference number (e.g., `GIG-ABA-98412`).
   - Tap **"Confirm Dispatch & Consign Waybill"**.
7. The customer receives an SMS and Email with their live tracking reference!

## 5. Understanding the 15-Minute Reservation & Quarantined Holds

### The 15-Minute Lock:
When a customer reaches checkout, the store temporarily **locks their chosen sizes for 15 minutes**. Nobody else can take them while they enter their debit card or bank transfer PIN.

### What is a "Quarantined Hold"?
If a customer takes longer than 15 minutes to pay (for example, their bank network delayed their transfer for an hour), their reservation expires.
- If someone else already bought that last dress in the meantime, the system **does not oversell or cancel silently**.
- It safely marks the order as **"Quarantined (Inventory Conflict)"** and flags it on your dashboard.
- You can simply open the order, check your Aba fabric cutting room if you can sew another piece, or tap **Refund** to return their money cleanly.

## 6. How to Handle Delivery Quote Requests

Some customers order from remote destinations or order 10+ bridesmaid dresses in bulk:
1. They select **"Request Delivery Quote"** at checkout instead of standard courier.
2. Their request appears on your Admin Home screen under **"Delivery Quotes Awaiting Price"**.
3. Tap **"Send Quote"** next to their name. This opens WhatsApp with their details pre-filled.
4. You give them the exact waybill price and send them their secure checkout link.

## 7. Changing Announcement Banners & Showroom Details

1. In the admin menu, tap **"Website"**.
2. **Top Announcement Bar:** Change the message (e.g., *"Festive Ready-to-Wear Collection Now Live • Free Aba Pickup"*). You can also turn it on or off.
3. **Showroom Hours & Phone:** Update your WhatsApp concierge number or showroom opening hours here anytime.
4. Tap **"Save & Publish Changes"** at the bottom.

## 8. Managing Delivery Fees & Discounts

### Delivery Pricing:
1. Tap **"Delivery"** in the admin menu.
2. You can update your shipping rates in Naira for:
   - Aba Metropolis Dispatch
   - South-East Interstate Express (Enugu, Owerri, Onitsha, Awka)
   - Lagos, Port Harcourt & Abuja Corridor
   - Nationwide Interstate Waybill
3. Tap **"Save Delivery Pricing"**.

### Discount Codes:
1. Tap **"Discounts"** in the admin menu.
2. Tap **"+ Create Code"** to make a new coupon (e.g., `CHRISTMAS15` for 15% off).
3. You can pause or reactivate coupon codes with a single tap.
